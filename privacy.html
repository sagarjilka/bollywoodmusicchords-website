Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

In short: we collect only what we need to run the app: your name and email for your account, and a record of which charts you open. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps or websites. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Who we are

Bollywood Music Chords ("we", "us") is run by Sagar Jilka in the United Kingdom. We are responsible (the "controller") for the personal information described in this policy. Contact us at sagarjilka@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Bollywood Music Chords mobile app (on iPhone, iPad and Android, including test versions shared through TestFlight and Google Play testing) and the website at bollywoodmusicchords.com.

What we collect

InformationWhy we use it
Account details: your name, email address and a securely stored (hashed) password or invite code. To create your account, sign you in and contact you about the app, for example pilot updates or a problem with your account.
Activity in the app: when you sign in and which chord charts you open. To understand which songs pilot users use and what to improve, and to protect our charts from copying.
Technical information: your device's IP address, app version and the time of each request, recorded automatically by our servers. To keep the service running and secure, and to fix problems.
Messages you send us, such as feedback or support emails, and feedback you send through TestFlight or Google Play. To reply to you and improve the app.

Your name may appear as a faint watermark on charts while you view them. This discourages copying and is visible only on your screen.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, and we do not use advertising or cross-app tracking tools.

Screenshot protection

To protect our charts, the app may block or detect screenshots and screen recordings while a chart is open. This happens on your device. No screenshot or recording is sent to us.

Our legal basis

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR), we use your information:

  • to provide the service you signed up for (contract);
  • to keep the service secure, protect our content and improve the app (legitimate interests, balanced against your rights);
  • where the law requires us to keep or share information (legal obligation).

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We share it only with companies that help us run the service, and only as far as they need to do their job:

  • Render: hosts our app server.
  • Neon: hosts our database.
  • GitHub: hosts this website.
  • Apple and Google: deliver the app and test versions to you. Their own privacy policies cover what they collect when you use the App Store, TestFlight or Google Play.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our users or the public.

International transfers

Some of our providers store or process data outside the UK, for example in the United States or the European Union. When they do, we rely on safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or adequacy regulations.

How long we keep it

  • Account details: while your account is open. When you delete your account, or once the pilot ends and you don't continue, we delete them within 90 days.
  • Activity in the app: up to 12 months, then deleted or made anonymous.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days.
  • Emails and feedback: as long as needed to deal with them, and no longer than 2 years.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct, delete or download your information, or object to or limit how we use it. Email sagarjilka@gmail.com and we'll reply within one month. To delete your account, see Delete your account.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We'd appreciate the chance to put it right first.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Information sent between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only in hashed form, and access to our systems is limited to the people who need it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change matters to how we use your information, we'll tell you in the app or by email.
