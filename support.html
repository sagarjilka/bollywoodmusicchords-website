Spotted a mistake in a chart?

Tell us the song, the bar number (shown at the top of each bar) and what you think it should be. Every chart is checked by musicians, and we fix reported mistakes quickly.

Common questions

Why can't I take a screenshot of a chart?

Screenshots are blocked on chart screens to protect the work of the musicians who write the charts. You can still play from the app on stage or in rehearsal.

Can I change the key?

Yes. Use the transpose buttons on any chart to move it up or down, and every chord changes with it.

A song I want isn't there.

Email us the song and film. We add songs regularly and pilot requests help us decide what to chart next.
